# Explore

Research this topic systematically. The goal is a clear, honest picture of the landscape — not just the first answer you find.

---

## Step 1 — Frame the question

Before searching, clarify:

1. **What is the exact question?** Write it precisely. Fuzzy question → fuzzy answer.
2. **What do we already know?** Don't research what we already have. What's the starting point?
3. **What decision does this feed?** This determines depth. A final decision needs more rigor than an initial exploration.
4. **What would change our direction?** Focus on evidence that actually matters.

---

## Step 2 — Search broadly

Search for:
- Overview articles and summaries (to understand the landscape)
- Authoritative primary sources: original papers, official documentation, the people who built the thing
- Known alternatives and comparisons
- Recent developments (last 6-12 months) — the space may have changed
- Critical perspectives — what do skeptics say?

For each source, note: what it covers, how authoritative it is, how recent it is.

---

## Step 3 — Read and extract signal

For each significant source, extract:
- The core claim or finding
- What evidence supports it
- What it assumes (often unstated)
- What it does NOT cover

Don't just accumulate links. Extract signal. A source you've summarized is more useful than one you've bookmarked.

---

## Step 4 — Map known and unknown

```markdown
## Known — high confidence
- [Fact, with source]

## Known — lower confidence or conflicting sources
- [Claim + the conflict, with sources on both sides]

## Unknown — relevant gaps
- [What we couldn't find that we needed]
- [Questions the research raised but didn't answer]

## Out of scope
- [What we deliberately didn't research and why]
```

---

## Step 5 — Form a recommendation

Based on the research:

1. **Best option / answer** — what's the most defensible conclusion, and why?
2. **Key tradeoffs** — what do you give up with this choice?
3. **Confidence level** — how certain are you? What would change your answer?
4. **Next step** — is more research needed, or is this enough to decide and build?

---
